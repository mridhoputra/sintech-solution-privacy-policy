Privacy Policy

Belajar Zat dan Perubahannya

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Summary: The app does not require an account and does not ask for the user's name, email address, phone number, or location. Scores, best completion times, and sound preferences are stored locally on the device. When the device is connected to the internet, Google AdMob may process certain data to serve and measure ads.

1. About the app

Belajar Zat dan Perubahannya is a science education app that helps seventh-grade junior secondary school students learn about matter and its changes through visual learning materials, multiple-choice quizzes, and interactive challenges. The app is independently developed by Code Fairlight ("we," "us," or "our").

2. Information we do not request

The app does not provide registration or login and does not ask users to provide:

  • a name;
  • an email address;
  • a phone number;
  • a home address;
  • photos, contacts, microphone access, or precise location; or
  • payment information.

3. Data stored on the device

The app stores the following data locally on the user's device:

  • the highest score for each multiple-choice quiz level;
  • the best challenge completion time; and
  • sound-effect preferences.

This local data is not sent to a server operated by us. Users can delete it by clearing the app's data through their device settings or by uninstalling the app.

4. Advertising and third-party services

The app uses Google AdMob to display ads when the device is connected to the internet. To provide, secure, and measure ads, Google may process information such as the IP address, device information, diagnostic data, interactions with ads, and approximate general location inferred from the IP address. Google processes this information in accordance with its own policies and terms.

Information about how Google uses data from apps that use its services is available on the How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services page.

5. Children's privacy

The app is intended for school-age students and may be used by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly request personally identifiable information from children. Ad requests are configured for child-directed treatment and are limited to ad content suitable for general audiences. Interest-based advertising and remarketing are disabled for requests treated as originating from children.

If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information through a means outside the app's functionality, please contact us so that we can address it.

6. Consent and privacy choices

Where required by law in the user's region, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent or provide privacy choices before ads are loaded. The available choices may vary depending on the user's region and age. Eligible users can revisit their privacy choices through the privacy menu provided within the app.

7. Data use and retention

Local data is used only to remember the user's achievements and preferences while the app remains installed. For data processed by Google AdMob, the purposes, legal basis, retention period, and user rights are governed by the applicable consent settings and Google's policies.

8. Security

We seek to limit data collection and use only third-party services relevant to the app's functionality. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. External links

The app may open external links, including the developer's portfolio or an advertiser's destination. Third-party sites and apps have their own privacy policies and are outside our control. Users and their parents or guardians are encouraged to review those policies before providing any information.

10. User rights and requests

Users or their parents or guardians may contact us with questions about the app's privacy practices. Because we do not operate user accounts or a user database, we generally do not possess device-local data that we can access or delete remotely. Choices related to advertising data are managed through the app's privacy message and the relevant Google services.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the app, advertising services, or legal requirements. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
